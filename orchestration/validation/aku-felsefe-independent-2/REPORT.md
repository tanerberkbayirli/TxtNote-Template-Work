### FAIL — direct Word paragraph spacing differs from the explicit 6/6 rule

The official guide requires **6 pt before and 6 pt after each body paragraph** (thesis p28–34; seminar p31–37). In the pre-correction DOCX render specimens, `word/document.xml` direct paragraph properties were:

- Thesis body-like paragraphs inspected: 38 total; 2 have `w:before=120` / `w:after=120` twips (6/6 pt), while **36 have `w:before=240` / `w:after=120` (12/6 pt)**.
- Seminar: 106 total; 1 has 120/120 twips, while **105 have 240/120 (12/6 pt)**.
- `w:line=360`, `w:lineRule=auto` on these body paragraphs (18 pt relative line value); run properties carry `Times New Roman`, `w:sz=24` (12 pt), and `w:ind firstLine=709` twips (12.506 mm).

Thus matching the editor's additive inter-paragraph gap does not satisfy the source's literal per-paragraph 6/6 properties. The DOCX setting is a supported-value mismatch; no semantic equivalence is inferred. The editor and Word PDF baselines from that specimen had matching page totals (19/30) and body/quote line pitch, but that does not cure the 12/6 XML. The template currently selects `paragraphSpacing: "additive"` in `aku-felsefe.js`; the shared fix is pending and has not been independently rechecked yet.
