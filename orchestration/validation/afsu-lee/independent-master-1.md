# AFSÜ LEE master export independent review

Date: 2026-10-02. Scope: source-backed spot audit of the official 2025 LEE guide/current spec and the official 2026 YL source template against `master-self-3.pdf` and its companion DOCX XML. No template, engine, or spec files were changed. PDF pages were rendered for visual inspection; Word COM was not used, so Word pagination remains pending.

## Results

- **PASS — specified DOCX geometry/properties; partial PDF geometry check.** The guide/spec requires A4, 25/40/25/25 mm page margins, 12 pt Times New Roman, justified body text, 1.5 spacing, zero first-line indent, and 6 pt before and after. Output DOCX has A4 sections; ordinary inner/body sections use `w:pgMar` 1417/2268/1417/1417 twips (top/left/bottom/right), and body paragraphs carry TNR 12 pt (`w:sz=24`), `w:jc=both`, `w:spacing before=120 after=120 line=360 lineRule=auto`, and `firstLine=0`. The cover section separately has 720-twip (12.7 mm) margins, matching the measured cover exception. In the inspected PDF body page p.13, content begins at x=40 mm; this spot check does not establish all page-edge geometry. PDF body text uses `/TimesNewRomanPSMT`; bold/italic variants are present, with embedded TrueType font files (`/FontFile2`). Evidence: `afsu-lee.json` rules; `master-self-3.pdf` p.13; `master-self-3.docx` `word/document.xml` section/paragraph properties.

- **PASS typography / FAIL case — heading hierarchy in this export.** The official guide specifies H1 14 pt bold, uppercase, centered and on a new page; H2 14 pt bold left, title case except conjunctions; H3 12 pt bold left, only the first word capitalized; H4+ 12 pt bold italic left and unnumbered, likewise sentence case. PDF p.13 `1. GİRİŞ` is centered, bold TNR 14; p.14 `2.1. Alt Başlık 1.1` is left/bold/14; p.15 `2.1.1. Alt Başlık 1.1.1` is left/bold/12; p.15 `Alt Başlık 1.1.1.1` is italic/12 and unnumbered. Thus size/weight/alignment/numbering follow the sampled rules, but the H3/H4 sample casing fails: `Alt Başlık` capitalizes the second word, while the guide says only the first word starts uppercase. Corresponding DOCX paragraphs use Heading1–Heading4 and expected TNR point sizes and bold/italic properties. Main chapter sequence is 1–6, with `7. KAYNAKLAR`; Arabic page 1 begins at GİRİŞ (physical p.13).

- **PASS — captions and table text, with continuation unknown.** The source requires 12 pt captions, table labels above and left aligned, figure labels below and centered, and only the number in bold; table cells are 10 pt. PDF pp.15–16 and 23 show those positions and sizes; the table-number fragments use the bold face while caption text is regular, and table cells are 10 pt TNR. The 45-row synthetic table continues on p.24: its full caption repeats and the column headings do not. The guide/spec does not establish continuation-label or repeated-header behavior, so that particular behavior remains UNKNOWN rather than a source-based failure.

- **PASS — abstracts and front/main numbering.** The guide requires each abstract to be 250+ words, at most one page, single spaced, with separate unlabeled aim/method/findings/conclusion paragraphs and 3–5 keywords. PDF p.6 has the Turkish abstract and four keywords on one page (fixture count about 314 words); p.7 has the English abstract and four keywords on one page (about 365 words). Neither contains citations. Page labels run `i` (p.6), `ii` (p.7), then `iii` at contents (p.8); GİRİŞ is `1` at p.13; sources are `20` at p.32; appendices continue as `21–22`, CV as `23`. The outer/inner covers and special pages p.3–4 are unnumbered, consistent with guide numbering beginning at ÖZET.

- **PASS — references and footnotes.** The guide specifies 10 pt TNR, single-spaced references, 6 pt between entries, current APA, and alphabetization. PDF p.32 has 10 pt TNR entries in alphabetical order; DOCX references have `w:sz=20`, single `line=240`, and `after=120` (6 pt). The sample italicizes book/journal titles. Footnote sample p.20 is 10 pt TNR at the page bottom with a separator; exact separator/numbering scope is UNKNOWN in the spec.

- **FAIL — approval/jury table rendering.** The official YL source template's Word-rendered p.4 has a clear, three-member table: one chair plus two members. `master-self-3.pdf` p.4 has three member rows, but their cell widths/borders are broken: header cells straddle the first row, right-edge borders protrude, and subsequent rows have inconsistent extents. This is visibly different from the official source page and makes the approval form difficult to read. Compare `orchestration/artifacts/afsu-lee/source/word-render/AFSU_Lisansustu_Yuksek_Lisans_Tez_Sablonu_2026.pdf` p.4 with this export p.4. The rest of the page follows the expected order: inner cover, ethics, approval, then acknowledgements/abstracts.

- **FAIL — YL outer-cover baseline.** Source evidence (`source/template-structure-evidence.json`, YL outer-cover p.1) measures the T.C. baseline at 176.1 pt from page top; output PDF p.1 is 177.675 pt (measured via text matrix), a +1.575 pt / +0.556 mm difference. Preserve this geometry failure; do not waive it as rendering tolerance.

- **FAIL — footer placement remains open.** Source `template-structure-evidence.json` measures the YL Roman `i` glyph at x=524.74 pt and baseline=797.04 pt from page top. Output `master-self-3.pdf` p.6 measures x=521.344 pt and baseline=804.094 pt: −1.198 mm horizontally and +2.488 mm vertically. Output footer text is 11 pt TNR and labels are continuous, but these coordinates fail source placement. The DOCX contains 19 section properties and PAGE fields in footer parts 6–19; a Word-rendered DOCX inspection is still needed to establish its effective footer appearance.

- **REVIEW — metadata field contract is masked by the fixture.** The official cover has a department line ending in “ANABİLİM DALI”; its approval sentence also appends “Anabilim Dalı” and “Programında” after the metadata fields. Fixture values already include “Eğitim Bilimleri Anabilim Dalı” and “Eğitim Programları ve Öğretim Programı”, so the generated approval page visibly duplicates “Anabilim Dalı” and “Programı”. A name-only value would instead omit the suffix on the cover unless the field/scaffold contract is clarified. Do not add suffixes automatically without resolving whether these fields mean a base name or the complete official label; correct the test fixture or descriptor contract accordingly.
