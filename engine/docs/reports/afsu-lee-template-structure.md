# AFSÜ LEE template structure evidence

This report records measured structure from the official copies under `Butun-Sablonlar/Ege/Afyonkarahisar Sağlık Bilimleri Üniversitesi/Lisansustu_Egitim_Enstitusu_Tez`. The DOCX originals were copied byte-for-byte to `orchestration/artifacts/afsu-lee/source/originals`; the listed SHA-256 values identify the inspected originals. DOCX structure is captured in `orchestration/validation/afsu-lee/source/docx-structure.json`; concise evidence is in `template-structure-evidence.json`. Word-rendered PDFs and the 2024 DOC COM inspection are kept under `orchestration/artifacts/afsu-lee/source/word-render`.

Both 2026 thesis masters specify page dimensions of 11906 × 16838 twips (about 210.0 × 297.0 mm) in OOXML; Word export measured 595.32 × 841.92 pt. The initial cover section uses 720 twips (1.27 cm) on each margin. The next section uses 1418 twips (2.50 cm) top, right, and bottom, with a 2268-twip (4.00 cm) left margin. The following sections retain those body margins. Header/footer distances are approximately 708–709 twips (1.25 cm). These are **MEASURED** source-document properties, not quoted guide requirements.

Word exports of both masters contain 27 A4 pages with the same sequence: outer cover; inner title/project page; scientific ethics; acceptance and approval; acknowledgements; Turkish abstract; English abstract; contents; abbreviations and symbols; table list; figure list; thesis body; CV. Pages 6–11 display lower-Roman i–vi. The body begins on PDF page 12 with Arabic 1 and continues through 16 on PDF page 27. The DOCX contains five section property blocks. Cover and front matter are separated by section breaks; the Roman front matter starts at i; body pagination restarts at 1.

The measured cover/footer details differ by degree. The doctorate master renders Arabic `1` on its cover, while the YL master’s cover has no page number. The official guide says numbering begins at ÖZET, so the doctorate cover `1` is a template artifact to suppress; both covers should be unnumbered. Both masters then render lower-Roman i–vi on PDF pages 6–11 and Arabic 1–16 on pages 12–27. Doctorate Roman glyphs are at x≈515–522 pt, top=758.2 pt; YL Roman glyphs at x≈522–527 pt, top=788.4 pt. Body Arabic glyphs are at top≈792.3 pt for doctorate and ≈788.4 pt for YL. The doctorate body section directly references footer5; the YL body section has no direct footer reference and inherits footer4. Both Word exports show the expected Arabic body sequence. The independent approval sheets also contain page fields (doctorate lower-Roman i; YL Arabic 1); these are standalone-sheet artifacts and should not be carried into the thesis pagination flow.

The standalone approval sheets are one-page A4 documents. Doctorate approval uses lower-Roman numbering starting at i; YL approval has `start=1` with number format unspecified and renders Arabic 1. Their jury tables are 6 × 3 and 4 × 3 including the header row. Thus they contain chair + four members (five jurors total) and chair + two members (three total), respectively, matching the guide’s 5/3 total-jury requirement when the advisor is counted within the jury. The first column labels the chair/member role; the second records title/name and affiliation; the third is for signatures. The separate 2026 sheets use the same row counts. Treat example names and affiliations as filler. Approval narrative runs are Times New Roman 12 pt with 1.5-line spacing (360 twips). Ordinary body example paragraphs in both thesis masters carry direct paragraph formatting: 360-twip (1.5-line) line spacing and 120-twip (6 pt) before and after. The abstract’s instructional paragraph separately specifies 12 pt, single line, 6 pt between paragraphs, and no first-line indent; retain that abstract-specific requirement. Do not rely on the linked `GvdeMetni` style alone: the directly formatted paragraphs establish the rendered body values. The Normal style is 11 pt with 276-twip automatic line spacing and is not the ordinary thesis body style.

Actual Word PDFs embed subset Times New Roman regular/bold/italic fonts across the masters and approval sheets. Calibri, Arial, Aptos, and Symbol also occur in small quantities in source examples/objects; do not infer that those are the required body font. The thesis covers contain DrawingML/VML drawing objects. On the outer cover, the top wave/logo group is page-anchored around x=37 pt, y=36 pt at approximately 526–529 × 118 pt. The bottom red band is anchored horizontally relative to the text column (not the page): offset 0.6–1.8 pt from the column origin, which renders at x≈36–562 pt; vertically it is page-anchored at y≈783–785 pt and measures approximately 523–525 × 29–31 pt. The source files use media relationships `rId10`–`rId12` for the top group fallback and `rId9`→`word/media/image1.emf` for the bottom band. The 2024 legacy cover-format DOC exports as two A4 pages, one per degree variant; Word COM’s `ComputeStatistics` returned three pages for that same file. Treat the COM statistic as renderer-dependent metadata, not a pagination requirement. Its measured section page size is 595.3 × 841.9 pt with 36-pt (0.5-inch) margins and 35.4-pt header/footer distances.

Measured cover, ethics, approval, abstract, and body block baselines plus exact drawing/footer anchors are recorded in `orchestration/validation/afsu-lee/source/template-structure-evidence.json`. Master cover page 1 uses the measured margin override 1.27 cm, while subsequent content sections use 2.50/4.00-cm margins; assess that cover-specific property against the official cover exception before implementation. The numbered doctorate cover and page-numbered standalone approvals conflict with the guide’s rule to start numbering at ÖZET; preserve them as source artifacts in the evidence, but follow the guide in the implementation. The 2024 DOC page-count discrepancy is metadata-only. No values have been borrowed from another university.
